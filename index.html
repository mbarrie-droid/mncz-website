About Minnesota Children's Zone

Minnesota Children's Zone is dedicated to providing youth, families, schools, and communities with educational support, leadership development opportunities, mentoring services, family engagement programs, and pathways to lifelong success.

Programs

School Success

Tutoring, literacy support, homework assistance, and STEM enrichment programs.

Teen Leadership Academy

Leadership development, entrepreneurship, civic engagement, and workforce readiness.

Mentoring

One-on-one and group mentoring that builds confidence and positive relationships.

College Readiness

Scholarship support, application assistance, SAT preparation, and college planning.

Career Development

Internships, career exploration, networking opportunities, and job readiness skills.

Family Engagement

Parent workshops, resource navigation, and community-building initiatives.
